Units for every kind of nerd

Each widget stacks two readouts you choose. Set your city and outfit once in the app, and every widget follows along.

°C °F K

Every classic scale

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin, plus Rankine, Réaumur, Rømer, Delisle and Newton.

ħ = c = G = kB = 1

Natural units

Today's temperature as a fraction of the Planck temperature, about 2×10⁻³⁰.

kBT

Thermal energy

The typical energy of a thermal kick, in meV. About 25 meV on a nice day.

ATP

Biology's currency

Thermal energy in units of ATP hydrolysis, its inverse, and the Boltzmann factor of one ATP, in scientific or log₁₀ form.

W/m²

Body heat loss

Radiation, convection and evaporation from your skin, using air temperature, humidity, wind and what you're wearing.

Download

Planck Weather isn't in the app stores yet. On Android you can install it directly. The iPhone and Apple Watch version is on the way.

Android phone

  1. On your phone, tap Download Planck Weather 1.1.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open the app, allow location (or type a city), and pick your clothing.
  4. Long-press your home screen → Widgets → Planck Weather. Choose the two units to stack.
